I'm a PhD student at **Zhejiang University**, driven by a question: **what gives rise to intelligence?**

My long-term research direction is **recursive self-improvement (RSI)**. I want to understand how intelligent systems could improve their own capabilities—and the processes that enable further improvement.

### Questions I want to explore

- How can systems discover and validate better ways to learn and reason?
- How can self-improvement be measured, and when does it generalize beyond the tasks used to evaluate it?
- What roles do memory, self-reference, and self-models play in intelligence?


### AI for Soul

**AI for Soul** is my personal theme for exploring minds, meaning, and self-reference. I'm especially drawn to Douglas Hofstadter's ideas about analogy, symbols, and strange loops.

Beyond research, I enjoy strength training, creative coding, and *Interstellar*.
